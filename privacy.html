Privacy Policy

Spritedex · Last updated 2 August 2026

The short version Spritedex does not collect your personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, and no tracking. The only thing it stores is which items you marked as owned.

Who we are

Spritedex is an unofficial, fan-made companion app for Fortnite players. It is not created by, endorsed by, affiliated with or sponsored by Epic Games, Inc.

Contact: laurent.koerge@me.com

What we store

Only two things, both created by you:

  • Which collectibles you marked as owned, and the date you marked each one.
  • Your app preferences — whether you finished onboarding, and whether analytics is enabled.

That's the complete list.

Where it is stored

On your device. If you are signed into iCloud, it also syncs through your own private iCloud database, managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

We do not operate a server that receives your collection. We cannot see it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, and have no way to collect:

  • Your name, email address, postal address or phone number
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar or health data
  • Your Fortnite or Epic Games account details
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or any tracking identifier
  • Your browsing or search history outside the app

Spritedex never connects to your game account. It cannot read what you own in-game. Everything in your collection is something you marked yourself.

Analytics

Analytics is off by default. You are never asked to enable it, and the app is fully functional with it off.

If you turn it on in Settings, we collect only anonymous crash reports and anonymous counts of which screens are used. These are never linked to your identity and never used for advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not use App Tracking Transparency because we do not track you. You can turn it off at any time.

Network requests

Spritedex makes two kinds of outbound request: downloading the catalogue of collectibles, and loading item images. Neither sends any information about you or your collection. Standard technical information (IP address, device type) is visible to those servers as with any web request. We do not log or store it.

Where item data comes from

Cosmetic item data is supplied by fortnite-api.com, a free public community API. Sprite data is maintained by hand from Epic Games' published update notes. Neither involves your personal data.

Children's privacy

Spritedex is rated 4+ and is safe for children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children either. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and there is no mechanism in the app by which a child could provide any.

Your rights and choices

  • See your data: it is visible in the app — it is your collection.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Delete my collection removes everything immediately, on your device and in iCloud. Deleting the app also removes local data.
  • Turn off analytics: Settings → Privacy.
  • Turn off sync: sign out of iCloud, or disable iCloud for Spritedex in iOS Settings.

Because we hold no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to export or delete on your behalf — you already have full control.

Legal bases (UK/EU users)

Where GDPR applies, our processing of app preference and optional analytics data rests on legitimate interests (operating and improving the app), and on your consent for analytics, which you give by enabling it and can withdraw at any time.

You have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, and to object to processing. As above, your data lives on your own device and iCloud account, so these rights are exercised directly through the app.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published here with a new date, and significant changes will be noted in the app.
