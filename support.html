Frequently asked

Why doesn't Spritedex fill in what I already own?

Because it can't, and no app can do this legitimately. There is no public, authorised way to read your Fortnite account's collection. Spritedex never connects to your game account — you mark items yourself. Any app claiming to import your locker automatically is doing something it shouldn't.

Is my collection saved if I get a new phone?

Yes, if you were signed into iCloud. Your collection syncs through your own private iCloud database and reappears when you sign in on the new device. If you weren't signed into iCloud, the data was only on the old device.

An item is missing, wrong, or has no picture.

Item data comes from a public community database that occasionally lags behind a game update. Pull down on any collection to refresh. If it's still wrong after a day or two, email us with the item name and we'll correct it.

Why do some items say “community reported”?

Spritedex labels where each entry came from. Anything we couldn't trace to an official Epic Games source is marked, so you know how much to trust it. We'd rather flag uncertainty than quietly present a guess as fact.

Why do some Sprites show “Unconfirmed” rarity?

Epic has named the Sprites but hasn't published which rarity each one is. Rather than guess, we show it as unconfirmed. It'll be filled in if Epic publishes it.

I marked something owned and it disappeared from the list.

You're probably viewing the “Missing” filter, which hides fully-owned items. Tap the filter icon and choose “All”. Nothing is ever deleted from your collection by an app update.

Does Spritedex work offline?

Yes. Once a collection has loaded it stays available with no connection, and the app works from the moment you install it without any network at all.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete my collection. It removes everything immediately, on your device and in iCloud. Deleting the app also removes local data. We hold nothing on any server, so there's nothing else to request.
